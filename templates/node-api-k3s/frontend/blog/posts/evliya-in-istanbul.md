# Evliya in Istanbul

Evliya Celebi walked Istanbul as if every street had a voice.

One morning near the water, he heard ferrymen calling, cups touching saucers, and a cat claiming the warm stone beside a coffee stall. He wrote only a little that day: the city is not a map first, it is a rhythm.

Neo Evliya keeps the same habit, but with a laptop in the bag.
